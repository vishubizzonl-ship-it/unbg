How it works

Three steps. No dialogue boxes.

  1. 01

    Drop it

    Drag a file, paste from the clipboard, or hand us a URL. JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and AVIF up to 25 MB.

  2. 02

    Cut it out

    Either the on-device model (private, no upload) or the cloud model (best edges on complex hair and glass).

  3. 03

    Take it

    Download a full-resolution PNG, apply a background preset, or push the whole batch to a ZIP.

Questions worth asking.

Is it really free without an account?

+

Yes. The tool runs without signup and the download is full resolution. A key only becomes necessary for batches above the free allowance or for API automation.

What does “on-device” mean exactly?

+

The segmentation model is downloaded once into your browser and executed locally with WebGPU (CPU fallback). Your image is not uploaded — you can confirm it in the network tab.

Do you keep my images?

+

On-device images never reach us. Cloud-mode images are deleted within 24 hours and are never used for training. There is no “contribute this image” prompt to mis-click.

Which formats and sizes are supported?

+

JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and HEIC up to 25 MB per image. Output: PNG with alpha, JPG, WebP or AVIF, with an optional target file size.

Why not just wait for the Canva migration?

+

Because credits, seats and platform lock-in are not a background remover. unbg stays a single-purpose tool: one image in, one clean cutout out.
